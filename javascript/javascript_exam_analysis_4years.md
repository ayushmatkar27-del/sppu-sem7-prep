## 💡 Summary of 4-Year University Paper Patterns

1. **Cycle Pattern Observed:**
   - **Set A (2022, 2023, 2026):** Nov 2022, May 2023, Nov 2023, and May 2026 share practically **identical questions** with minor order shifts.
   - **Set B (2024, 2025):** May 2024, Nov 2024, May 2025, and Nov 2025 introduced questions on *Mask Out, Function as Object, Stacks/Queues using Arrays, Advanced RegEx, Netscape 4 event model, and Location Object*.
2. **Fixed Code-Writing Questions (Guaranteed 18+ Marks):**
   - **Calculator Script** (Unit 6) $\rightarrow$ Repeated **7 times** (6 Marks)
   - **Student Form Validation Script** (Unit 6) $\rightarrow$ Repeated **6 times** (5/6 Marks)
   - **Object in Array Program** (Unit 3) $\rightarrow$ Repeated **5 times** (6 Marks)
   - **Change Background Color on Mouseover/Focus** (Unit 5) $\rightarrow$ Repeated **4 times** (6 Marks)
