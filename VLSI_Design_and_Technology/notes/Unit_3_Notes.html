🏆 Question 7: Clock Management Techniques in FPGA (DCM, PLL, BUFG)

Marks: 6 Marks | **Frequency:** 3x across 3 papers (40% Priority)

  • Global Clock Buffers (BUFG): High-drive internal clock buffers driving balanced, dedicated low-impedance H-tree routing tracks to maintain near-zero skew across millions of flip-flops.
  • Digital Clock Manager (DCM): Uses all-digital delay lines to cancel clock distribution delay ($T_{ ext{insertion}} = 0$) and provide precise 90°, 180°, 270° phase adjustments.
  • Phase-Locked Loop (PLL): Analog VCO circuit performing frequency synthesis (e.g. converting 50 MHz input to 400 MHz internal clock via $f_{ ext{out}} = f_{ ext{in}} \cdot rac{M}{D}$) and filtering out clock jitter.
