🎯 SPPU 8-Mark Scoring Blueprint:
• Need for DFT (Inaccessibility, Silicon Cost vs Test Cost, Exponential Sequential State Space) (2 Marks)
• Definitions and Differences: Controllability, Observability & Predictability (3 Marks)
• SCOAP Quantitative Testability Analysis ($CC_0, CC_1, CO$) with 2-Input AND Gate Example (3 Marks)

SCOAP Formulas for 2-Input AND Gate ($Y = A \cdot B$):

  • CC1(Y) = CC1(A) + CC1(B) + 1 (Both inputs must be 1 to make output 1)
  • CC0(Y) = min(CC0(A), CC0(B)) + 1 (Either input 0 makes output 0)
  • CO(A) = CO(Y) + CC1(B) + 1 (To observe A, side input B must be held at non-controlling 1)
