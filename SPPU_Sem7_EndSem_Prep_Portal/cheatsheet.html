A quick-memory sheet of all key formulas, S-matrices, lambda rules, and layer equations to review 1 hour before the exam.

📡 Radiation & Microwave Theory (RMT)

Units 3, 4, 5, 6 (End-Sem)
Rectangular Waveguide Cutoff & Guide Wavelength (Unit 3)
$$\lambda_c = \frac{2ab}{\sqrt{m^2 b^2 + n^2 a^2}} \xrightarrow{\text{TE}_{10}} 2a, \quad \lambda_g = \frac{\lambda_0}{\sqrt{1 - (f_c/f)^2}}$$
Dominant $\text{TE}_{10}$ cut-off is independent of height $b$. Guide wavelength $\lambda_g > \lambda_0$ inside waveguide.
Waveguide Velocity Relation & Wave Impedance (Unit 3)
$$v_p \cdot v_g = c^2, \quad v_p = \frac{c}{\sqrt{1 - (f_c/f)^2}} > c, \quad Z_{\text{TE}} = \frac{\eta}{\sqrt{1 - (f_c/f)^2}}$$
Phase velocity $v_p$ exceeds $c$ while energy velocity $v_g < c$. $\eta = 377\;\Omega$ is free-space wave impedance.
Microstrip Effective Dielectric Constant (Unit 3)
$$\varepsilon_{\text{eff}} = \frac{\varepsilon_r + 1}{2} + \frac{\varepsilon_r - 1}{2} \left( 1 + 12 \frac{h}{W} \right)^{-1/2}$$
Quasi-TEM mode with $1 < \varepsilon_{\text{eff}} < \varepsilon_r$. Strip width $W$, substrate thickness $h$, dielectric constant $\varepsilon_r$.
Magic Tee Ideal Scattering Matrix
$$\mathbf{S} = rac{1}{\sqrt{2}} egin{bmatrix} 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 \ 0 & 0 & 1 & -1 \ 1 & 1 & 0 & 0 \ 1 & -1 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$
Notice $S_{24} = -1$ (180° out of phase split in E-plane arm) and $S_{34} = 0$ (cross isolation).
Directional Coupler Parameters
$$C = 10 \log_{10}(P_1/P_3), \quad D = 10 \log_{10}(P_3/P_4), \quad I = C + D$$
Distance between two holes $d = \lambda_g / 4$. Path difference for backward wave is $2d = \lambda_g/2$ (180° cancellation).
Hull Cut-Off Magnetic Field ($B_c$)
$$B_c = rac{\sqrt{8 m V_0 / e}}{b (1 - a^2/b^2)}$$
For Cavity Magnetron: $a$ is cathode radius, $b$ is anode cavity radius, $V_0$ is DC anode voltage.
Radar Maximum Range Equation
$$R_{ ext{max}} = \left[ rac{P_t G^2 \lambda^2 \sigma}{(4\pi)^3 P_{r, ext{min}}} ight]^{1/4}$$
$P_t$ is peak transmit power, $G$ is antenna gain, $\sigma$ is radar cross-section, and $P_{r, ext{min}}$ is minimum detectable echo power.
VSWR Double Minimum Method (High SWR)
$$S pprox rac{\lambda_g}{\pi \Delta d} \quad ( ext{where } P = 2 P_{ ext{min}})$$
$\Delta d = |d_1 - d_2|$ is distance between twice minimum points; eliminates detector saturation errors.
Specific Absorption Rate (SAR) Formula
$$ ext{SAR} = rac{\sigma |E|^2}{ ho} \quad [ ext{W/kg}]$$
$\sigma$ is tissue conductivity, $E$ is internal electric field, $ ho$ is tissue mass density. Standard consumer limit = 1.6 W/kg.

⚡ VLSI Design & Technology (Course Code: 404182)

Units 3, 4, 5, 6 (End-Sem)
CPLD vs FPGA Architectural Metrics (Unit 3)
$$\text{CPLD: Macrocell (Wide AND-OR)} \quad \longleftrightarrow \quad \text{FPGA: CLB (4/6-input LUT)}$$
CPLD: Pin-to-pin propagation delay $t_{pd}$ is 100% deterministic, non-volatile (EEPROM/Flash), low standby power. FPGA: SRAM-based volatile, segmented routing delay dependent on place-and-route, ultra-high gate capacity (>100k gates).
CMOS Aspect Ratio (W/L) Sizing Rules (Unit 4)
$$\left(\frac{W}{L}\right)_p \approx 2.5 \cdot \left(\frac{W}{L}\right)_n, \quad W_{\text{series}} = k \cdot W, \quad W_{\text{parallel}} = W$$
Because electron mobility $\mu_n \approx 2.5 \mu_p$, PMOS channel width must be sized 2.5 times NMOS width for symmetric rise and fall propagation delays ($t_{pLH} = t_{pHL}$). Series transistors scale width by $k$ to maintain unit driving resistance.
Short-Channel Effects: DIBL & Velocity Saturation (Unit 4)
$$\Delta V_{th} = -\eta \cdot V_{DS}, \quad I_{D,\text{sat}} = W \cdot C_{ox} \cdot v_{\text{sat}} \cdot (V_{GS} - V_{th})$$
In short-channel MOSFETs ($L < 0.25\,\mu\text{m}$), drain bias lowers source barrier (DIBL), while velocity saturation clamps carrier speed at $v_{\text{sat}} \approx 10^7\text{ cm/s}$, collapsing quadratic saturation current to linear.
Mead-Conway Lambda (λ) Rules Summary (Unit 5)
$$\text{Poly: } 2\lambda, \quad \text{Diff: } 2\lambda, \quad \text{Metal-1: } 3\lambda, \quad \text{Contact Cut: } 2\lambda \times 2\lambda$$
Polysilicon gate extension beyond active diffusion = $2\lambda$. Metal overlap of contact cut = $1\lambda$. Active-to-active spacing = $3\lambda$. Allows layout to scale seamlessly with technology shrink factor $\lambda$.
LFSR State Count & JTAG Boundary Scan Pins (Unit 6)
$$N_{\text{states}} = 2^n - 1 \quad \text{(Maximal LFSR)}, \quad \text{JTAG: TCK, TMS, TDI, TDO, TRST*}$$
LFSR characteristic polynomial generates $2^n-1$ non-zero pseudo-random vectors for BIST. IEEE 1149.1 JTAG uses 4 mandatory pins (and 1 optional TRST*) with a 16-state TAP FSM to test chip interconnects and boundary scan registers.

🧠 Deep Learning

Units 3, 4, 5, 6
Convolutional Output Spatial Dimension
$$W_{ ext{out}} = \left\lfloor rac{W_{ ext{in}} - K + 2P}{S} ight floor + 1$$
$W_{ ext{in}}$ = input size, $K$ = kernel size, $P$ = padding, $S$ = stride. MaxPool output follows identical equation without padding.
Batch Normalization Forward Transform
$$\hat{x}_i = rac{x_i - \mu_B}{\sqrt{\sigma_B^2 + \epsilon}}, \quad y_i = \gamma \hat{x}_i + eta$$
Normalizes activations across mini-batch; learnable parameters $\gamma$ and $eta$ restore network representational power.
LSTM Cell Update & Gate Equations
$$C_t = f_t * C_{t-1} + i_t * ilde{C}_t, \quad h_t = o_t * anh(C_t)$$
$f_t$ = Forget gate, $i_t$ = Input gate, $ ilde{C}_t$ = Candidate state, $o_t$ = Output gate. Asterisk $(*)$ denotes Hadamard element-wise product.
VGG 3x3 Parameter Reduction Ratio
$$ ext{Three } 3 imes3 = 27 C^2 \quad ext{vs} \quad ext{One } 7 imes7 = 49 C^2 \implies 45\% \downarrow$$
Three stacked $3 imes3$ convolutions cover identical $7 imes7$ receptive field with 45% fewer parameters and 3 non-linear ReLUs.
