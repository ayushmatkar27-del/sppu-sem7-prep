# SPPU Sem 7 (2019 Course Pattern) - End-Sem Preparation Hub

A unified web portal and study resource directory designed specifically for SPPU Engineering students preparing for their 7th Semester End-Sem Examinations.

## 🌐 Opening the Website Portal

To open the complete study portal:
1. Double-click or open `index.html` in your browser:  
   👉 [SPPU_Sem7_EndSem_Prep_Portal/index.html](file:///d:/College/End%20sem%202026%20nov%20dec/SPPU_Sem7_EndSem_Prep_Portal/index.html)
2. Works 100% offline without needing any local server or internet connection.
3. Automatically references all live notes and PDFs in your subject folders.

---

## ⚡ Live Synchronization Feature
The portal uses direct relative paths:
- Whenever you or anyone edits or modifies files in `Radiation_and_Microwave_Theory/notes/`, `VLSI_Design_and_Technology/notes/`, `Cloud_Computing/notes/`, `Deep_Learning/notes/`, or `javascript/notes/`, the changes **immediately reflect** on the portal without needing to rebuild anything.

## 📚 Subject Coverage

1. **Radiation & Microwave Theory (RMT)**: Tees, Directional Couplers, Magnetrons, TWT, Solid-state Diodes (Gunn/IMPATT/PIN), Hazards & Radar.
2. **VLSI Design & Technology**: CPLD vs FPGA, CMOS Logic Sizing, Lambda Design Rules, Stick Diagrams, BIST & JTAG Testing.
3. **Cloud Computing**: Hardware Virtualization, Hypervisors, CIA Security Model, CSA Cloud Threats, AWS & GAE Services, IoT Integration.
4. **Deep Learning (Elective IV)**: Batch Normalization, Activations & Gradients, VGGNet vs AlexNet, DenseNet, LSTM Cells, Transfer Learning.
5. **JavaScript (Elective III)**: Functions & Closures, Objects, Regular Expressions, DOM Manipulation, AJAX & ES6+.
